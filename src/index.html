    How to play

    Pick a chip, then tap the board: a number, a dozen, a column (2:1), or red, black, odd, even, 1–18 or 19–36. Undo takes back your last chip and Clear empties the board. Place bets, and your wallet signs every chip as one bet. Your chips stay on the board after the spin, to bet again or change.

    One wheel serves everyone at the table. It spins every 20 seconds, whether or not anybody bets, and takes no bets in the last 3 seconds before each spin. A number returns 36 for 1, a dozen or a column 3 for 1, and the rest 2 for 1. Zero is the house's: that is the whole 2.7% edge.

    Your stake goes to the game's developer, who runs the wheel and pays the winners: you trust them to pay. Where the ball lands is fixed before anyone bets, and this page checks every spin against the casino's records, and shows it if a spin paid you short.
