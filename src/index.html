How it works

Click a number or an outside bet to put a chip on it; right-click takes one off. All your chips are one bet, which your wallet signs whole: a number returns 36 for 1, a dozen or a column 3 for 1, and red, black, odd, even, low and high 2 for 1. Zero belongs to the house, which is the game's whole 2.7% edge.

Everyone at the table shares one spin, and your bet is a developer bet: its stake goes to the game's developer, who runs the wheel, and you trust them to pay. Before anybody bets, the casino names the spin's round, the hash of a secret, and the wheel publishes the hash of its seed; your bet names both, and the casino records them with it, so where the ball lands is fixed before your bet is in, and nobody can change it. Twenty seconds after the first chip at the table is down, the wheel covers every layout on the spin with one casino bet of them all against the casino's bankroll, which reveals the secret and the seed, and pays each covered bet what its chips win there. This page works out the number from the secret and the seed, checks both against the hashes your bet named, and shows any shortfall, or what your chips would have won if the wheel did not cover them; anyone can check every spin against what the casino records. A bet the wheel does not cover, one that comes too late for its spin or a spin the bankroll turns down, gets its stake back. Each asset has its own wheel: you join the one for what your wallet plays with.
